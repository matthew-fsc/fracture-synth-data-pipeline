Meeting Transcript - System Requirements Discussion
Date: 2024-01-15
Participants: John Smith (CTO), Sarah Johnson (Operations Manager), Mike Davis (IT Director)

John: Thanks everyone for joining. We need to discuss our operational system challenges.

Sarah: Yes, we're seeing significant delays in handoffs between departments. On average, tickets are taking 24-48 hours to move from IT to Operations.

Mike: The handoff failure rate is around 12%. That's causing a lot of rework and customer complaints.

John: What about our ticket volume?

Sarah: We're processing about 500 tickets daily, with monthly volumes around 15,000. The system is struggling to keep up.

Mike: We also have compliance issues. Last month we had 8 compliance failures related to data handoffs not being properly tracked.

John: How many advisors are we working with?

Sarah: We have about 25 advisors across different departments, but coordination is a challenge.

Mike: The realtime ingestion is also a problem. We're getting about 10,000 events per day, and the system can't process them fast enough.

Sarah: Workflow stalls are happening about 15-20 times per month. This is impacting our SLA compliance.

John: So we need a system that can handle:
1. Faster handoffs between departments
2. Better tracking and compliance
3. Real-time processing of high-volume events
4. Improved coordination with advisors
5. Reduced workflow stalls

Mike: Exactly. We need something that can scale with our growth and maintain compliance.

Sarah: And it needs to integrate with our existing department systems.

John: Let's prioritize this as high urgency. We need to start planning the requirements.
